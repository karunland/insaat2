PROCESS

How does it work?

Six steps from application to handover. At every step you know what is happening and who is doing what.

  1. +15.00

    First meeting and site visit

    We review your building, title deeds and expectations together.

  2. +12.00

    Risk assessment

    We support you through the assessment carried out by licensed bodies under Law No. 6306.

  3. +9.00

    Owners' meeting and agreement

    Share ratios, flat layouts and the delivery schedule are presented openly to all owners; contracts are signed.

  4. +6.00

    Design and permits

    Architectural, structural and MEP designs are prepared and the building permit is obtained.

  5. +3.00

    Demolition and construction

    The building is safely demolished and rebuilt to the 2018 Turkish Seismic Code, with regular progress reports.

  6. ±0.00

    Turnkey handover

    The occupancy permit is obtained and flats are handed over to the owners.

WHY RENEW?

Same earthquake, two different buildings.

Buildings constructed under old codes, with ground floors opened up for shops, are among the most damaged in earthquakes. See the difference yourself.
