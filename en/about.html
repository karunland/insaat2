SHEET 01 ABOUT

We deliver what we promise, on the day we promised.

MAKSEF is an Istanbul construction company that delivers infrastructure and building works in public tenders, and builds new homes together with owners in urban renewal projects.

The same rigour on site and on paper.

Working with public institutions means following the specification to the letter, tracking the schedule day by day and backing every progress payment with documentation. We bring the same discipline to our urban renewal projects.

Our experience ranges from wastewater and drinking water lines to the repair and seismic retrofit of hospital and education buildings.

In urban renewal, our job is not only to build: from risk assessment to handover, we build a transparent relationship in which every owner knows where the process stands.
